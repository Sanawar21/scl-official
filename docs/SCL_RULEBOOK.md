# SCL Season 2 — Official Rule Book

**Section-C Cricket League · Season 2 · Official Rules & Regulations · V2.0**

_This rule book is the binding document for all teams, players, and managers. It is
supplemented by three operational guides: the **Vault Guide**, the **Wagers Guide**, and
the **Economy Guide**. Where the platform and this document disagree, the platform is the
source of truth for mechanics; this document is the source of truth for rules._

---

## 1. Tournament Format & Standings

### 1.1 Season structure
- **12-match Double Round Robin** — every team plays every other team twice.
- **No final.** The team that tops the table after all 12 matches **wins the season
  outright** and is crowned **League Champion**.

### 1.2 Points system
| Result | Points |
|---|---|
| Win | **2** |
| Draw / tie | **1** |
| Loss | **0** |

### 1.3 Tie-breakers (in order)
1. **Net Run Rate (NRR)**
2. **Head-to-head** record between the tied teams
3. **Total boundaries** scored across the season

### 1.4 Administration & neutrality
- The **President is now an active manager**, so an **external, unbiased member is added
  to the committee** to resolve disputes and keep the league fair.

## 2. The Draft & Player Tiers

### 2.1 Auction order (Season 2)
```
Platinum Players → Gold Players → 5-minute Trade Break → Silver Players
```
- Bidding happens **tier by tier**; the auction engine exposes one phase at a time.
- A **5-minute trade break** sits between the Gold and Silver phases.
- **Phase B** (after Silver) is the final chance to fill incomplete rosters at a fixed
  price (see the Economy Guide).

### 2.2 Tiers, base prices & credits
| Tier | Base price | Draft credits per player |
|---|---|---|
| **Platinum** | 3,000 | 3 |
| **Gold** | 2,000 | 2 |
| **Silver** | 1,000 | 1 |

- Each team has a **total of 8 draft credits**. A player's credits are deducted when you
  win them; unspent credits refund **1,000 each** at the end of the draft.
- **Bid increments** are fixed at 50 (i.e. bids must rise in 50-unit steps from the base
  or the current bid).
- A team roster is **4 players**: the **manager (their own player)** plus **3 bought
  players**.

### 2.3 Mandatory rosters
- If players remain **unsold after the draft**, admins will **forcefully assign** them to
  incomplete teams so every team fields a full roster.

## 3. Teams & The Season

### 3.1 Team identity
- Teams are **persistent** across seasons — name, logo, and about section live with the
  team, not the season. A team that is not playing this season still exists and is
  managed from its owner's account.
- **Every player gets the universal 10,000 funding** before the S2 auction. There is
  **no tier purse** — a team's money **is its manager's wallet**.
- **Pre-auction balancing grants:** admins may gift **weaker managers extra funds before
  the auction** (on top of the 10k) to level the field — at the committee's discretion,
  case by case, recorded with a comment in the ledger.

### 3.2 Umpiring duty — every team must contribute
- **Every team must volunteer to umpire at least 3 matches** across the season.
- The committee keeps an umpiring log. If a team has **not fulfilled its 3-match quota
  by the end of the season, a fine of 1,500 is charged** to the team's wallet.

### 3.3 Match rewards
- Each **finalized match** credits **250 to every player's wallet** (not just the two
  teams that played) — this is the fuel that keeps the economy liquid. See the Economy
  Guide.

## 4. Player Movement

### 4.1 Substitution / release clause
- A team may **release a player** from its squad under the **Substitution Release Clause**:
  - The releasing team pays the **player's release value = 50% of the price the player
    was sold at in the auction** (their auction price).
  - The released player becomes available again (admin handles the re-allocation).
- This is a **financial** mechanism — it does not bypass Phase B or trade windows.

### 4.2 Trades
- During the **trade break** (and any open trade window), managers may propose swaps of
  players **with optional cash in either direction**. Both sides must accept.

## 5. Discipline & On-Field Conduct

### 5.1 Field invasions
- **Fine of 500** to the **manager of the team** whose player (or any non-playing team
  member) **invades the field of play**. Applies per incident, whether the match is live
  or in between overs.

### 5.2 The Kela Protocol
- For **recurring disputes or repeated violations**, management reserves the right to
  **strip a manager of team authority and hand control to the team's players**. The admin
  dashboard's takeover/restore tool is the enforcement mechanism.

### 5.3 Match & market integrity
- **Match-fixing and collusion are zero-tolerance.** The admin team monitors for
  suspicious play or unnatural betting volumes; compromised markets are **voided
  immediately and all stakes refunded** (see the Wagers Guide).

## 6. Marketing & Announcements

### 6.1 Sponsored match announcements
- A manager may **sponsor a match announcement** on the official announcements group for
  **200** — used to **promote their upcoming match, or (in good spirit) to talk trash**.
- Payment is taken from the manager's wallet at the time of posting; the admin confirms
  and posts on the manager's behalf.
- Content stays within the league's code of conduct — abusive or defamatory posts may be
  removed without refund.

## 7. Money, the Vault & Prediction Markets (summary)

Full mechanics live in the three guides; the essentials:

- **Wallet** — every player has one; for managers it is also the team's money.
- **Vault** — lock funds for a **7% compounded yield per match**; choose auto-reinvestment
  or manual harvest. Locked capital is untouchable until the end of Match 12. (Vault Guide)
- **Prediction markets** — pooled Yes/No markets with **fair-odds House guarantee**:
  the House automatically tops up so winners always get at least fair odds. (Wagers Guide)
- **Auto mode** — if you don't want to manage your finances, everything that comes in
  goes straight to your vault automatically. (Economy Guide)
