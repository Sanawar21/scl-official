# SCL Branding Prompt — for Nano Banana

_Paste the block below into Nano Banana (or any AI image/branding tool) to generate the
SCL identity. It is written to produce a minimal, modern, professional-league look._

---

## Prompt

Create a complete brand identity for **SCL — Section-C Cricket League**, a professional
amateur cricket league (12-match double round robin season, 4-8 teams, young adult players
and managers). The identity must feel like a **modern professional sports league** — think
Premier League / IPL clean minimalism, not clip-art or cartoon.

**Logo (primary mark)**
- A single, memorable mark: a stylized cricket ball integrated with an abstract "SCL" wordmark
  or monogram. Avoid literal clip-art bats or grounds.
- Flat, vector-style, geometric; works at 16px (favicon) and 400px (banner) without losing
  form. No gradients heavier than a subtle two-tone; no drop shadows.
- Font: bold geometric sans-serif (e.g. Montserrat / Sora / Space Grotesk style), tight
  letter-spacing, uppercase "SCL" with "SECTION-C CRICKET LEAGUE" as a small wordmark below.

**Color system**
- Primary: deep navy or midnight green (professional, cricket-grass adjacency).
- Accent: one electric accent (gold/amber or neon green) used sparingly for the ball seam
  or the league line.
- Neutral: off-white and charcoal for type and backgrounds. 2-3 color variants of the logo:
  full color, single-color (white-on-dark), and single-color (dark-on-light).

**Deliverables / variants**
1. Primary logo (full color, horizontal lockup).
2. Logo mark only (for avatar / app icon / favicon).
3. Wordmark only (for scoreboard / video overlays).
4. One hero banner: logo centered on a dark navy field with a subtle cricket-ball-seam
   texture or a thin gold rule line.
5. One social avatar (square, 1:1).

**Tone**
Minimal. Confident. Zero ornament. The league is modernizing — it runs its finances,
auction, and predictions on a platform — so the identity should feel tech-native, not
retro. No mascots, no faces, no stadiums, no realistic textures.

---

## Notes for iteration

- If the first pass is too literal (bat/ball clip-art), ask for: "abstract geometric monogram
  with a single cricket-ball seam detail".
- If it feels dated, ask for: "flatter, bolder geometry, more negative space".
- Keep the gold accent to ONE element — the logo should stay two-tone dominant.
- The favicon must read as "S" at small sizes; test it scaled down before accepting.
