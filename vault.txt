               THE VAULT

            SCL Season 2: Official Mechanics & Rules

Overview

The Vault is an official banking mechanic for SCL Season 2. It offers a risk-free alternative to the
prediction market, allowing managers to lock away capital to secure a compounded war chest for
the next season's draft.

   "Choose between total liquidity for betting or guaranteed wealth for the future."

Core Rules

1. The Deposit & Lock
Deposits are converted from "Liquid Cash" (betting funds) to "Locked Capital." Once moved, the
principal is untouchable until the end of Match 12.

2. Yield & Reinvestment Choice
The Vault generates 7% yield per match.

     · Default (Compounding): Profits are automatically added to the principal, increasing the yield
       for the next match.

     · Manual Harvest: Managers have the option to not reinvest match profits. In this case, 7% is
       calculated only on the initial principal each match.

3. The Iron Lock (Standard)
Unless harvested (if profits are not reinvested), all funds remain strictly locked until the season
conclusion. There are no emergency withdrawals.
