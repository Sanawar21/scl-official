SCL Powered by Chandia CC

Official Technology Partnership & Sponsorship - Section-C Cricket League.

Announcement

Effective {{ announced_at }}, the Section-C Cricket League entered into an official technology partnership and sponsorship agreement with Chandia Cricket Club. Chandia CC provides the full technical ecosystem for SCL - website, auction platform, stats tracking, and match management - and in return receives prominent dual-brand recognition across every SCL asset.

The SCL wordmark is shown alongside Chandia CC as the league's technology sponsor on the website, scoreboards, broadcast graphics, social media, and the browser favicon.

Agreement at a glance

TermThree (3) consecutive tournament seasons, commencing {{ announced_at }}
ServicesCore website infrastructure, auction technology, stats tracking system, and ongoing maintenance
Recognition"SCL powered by Chandia CC" branding on all tournament assets, digital platforms, media channels, and public broadcasts
Dual-brand lockupOfficial branding featuring Chandia CC accents within the primary SCL lockup, shown on scoreboards, overlays, and broadcasts
AssetsSCL wordmark shown with Chandia CC on hero banners, social avatars (1:1), app icons, and favicons
AppreciationA dedicated appreciation post recognizing Chandia CC's contribution is published every season, without exception
League controlSCL Management retains full authority over league policy, rules, and schedules. The sponsorship confers no operational control or board votes.

Why Chandia CC

For the two seasons prior to this agreement, Chandia Cricket Club provided the entire SCL technical ecosystem on a voluntary basis. This agreement formalizes that relationship for the upcoming three-season term with full branding recognition under Option B (Strategic Sponsorship & Branding Recognition).

Every feature you use on this platform - the auction room, real-time live board, match scorer, league tables, leaderboards, vault, and wagers - is delivered and maintained by Chandia CC as SCL's technology partner.

Season appreciation

This page serves as the standing public acknowledgment of the partnership. A dedicated appreciation post recognizing Chandia CC's technological contribution is published on this platform at the conclusion of every season, without exception.

Current partnership status: active
